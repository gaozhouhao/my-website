## Portfolio typography

Before changing visible content or layout, read `docs/typography.md`.

- Do not leave only one or two Chinese characters on the final line of a heading or paragraph. Use the shared `ReadingText` component and inspect responsive wrapping after changes.
- Keep technical names intact, including RISC-V, ResNet-8, and 2-bit. Preserve official paper and patent titles.
- Organize long text into overview, work, results, and validation scope before styling it.
- Preserve the quiet editorial direction: modest serif headings, readable body text, generous spacing, and restrained purple accents. Do not enlarge section headings or fill whitespace with additional boxes.
